12 Fundamental Vector Operations

Vector Addition

Combining two vectors to form a resultant vector.

A + B = (Ax+Bx, Ay+By)

Vector Subtraction

Finding the difference between two vectors.

A - B = (Ax-Bx, Ay-By)

Scalar Multiplication

Scaling a vector by a real number.

k · A = (k·Ax, k·Ay)

Scalar Division

Dividing a vector by a scalar value.

A / k = (Ax/k, Ay/k)

Normalization

Converting a vector to unit length while preserving direction.

Â = A / ||A||

Magnitude

The length or size of a vector.

||A|| = √(Ax² + Ay²)

SqrMagnitude

Squared length of a vector (faster to compute).

||A||² = Ax² + Ay²

Dot Product

Measures the similarity of two vectors.

A · B = AxBx + AyBy

Cross Product

Measures the perpendicularity of two vectors.

A × B = AxBy - AyBx

Projection (A on B)

The component of A that lies along B.

projBA = [(A·B) / ||B||²] B

Projection (B on A)

The component of B that lies along A.

projAB = [(A·B) / ||A||²] A

Reflection

Reflecting a vector over another vector.

refBA = 2projBA - A
